Our standards

Three standards, for every tool and every client.

Standard 1

Transparent automation.

Callers and customers always know when they are dealing with an automated system.

  • Every call and message discloses that it is automated
  • The business it represents is named at the start
  • Applied on every channel we run for you
Standard 2

Consent and opt-out.

We only contact people who have agreed to hear from you, and they can withdraw at any time.

  • Only contacts who have opted in are messaged
  • Every message includes a way to stop
  • Opt-out requests honoured within one working day
Standard 3

Data protection.

Your customers' data is handled under a written agreement and stays yours.

  • A one-page data processing agreement
  • Hosted in Malaysia or Singapore
  • Exportable in full if you leave
ChannelsEmail first. Summaries, follow-ups and reports go by email. WhatsApp is a separate service we can add, on a business number we set up and run. SMS only for plain reminders.
LanguagesEnglish and Bahasa Malaysia. The Malay voice goes live only after you approve a recorded test call.
