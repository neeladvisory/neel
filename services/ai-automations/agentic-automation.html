How an agent works

One enquiry, handled end to end.

Example: the sales workflow at a training provider, from a new enquiry to a logged follow-up.

  1. 1 · TriggerA reader emails to ask about group rates for a course.
  2. 2 · Reads the recordThe agent checks the CRM for their history and your published rates.
  3. 3 · Does the taskIt replies in English or BM, within the rules you set.
  4. 4 · Your approvalA discount outside your rules waits for your yes before it is offered.
  5. 5 · Follow-upIt follows up the quote and logs every step in the CRM.
