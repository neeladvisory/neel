A Team That Never Clocks Out

Agentic AI
Automation.

AI agents that run the busywork, across the business. Five agents cover marketing, sales, operations, finance and customer support. They share one CRM, pass work to each other, and you approve anything that goes out.

Put the agents to work Meet the team ↓

The Team

One business, five agents.

Each agent owns one part of the business. All five read from and write to the same CRM, so nobody asks the customer the same question twice.

Shared Memory

One record, one memory.

Lead forms, emails, WhatsApp messages, calls, quotations, sales orders and payments land in one CRM. Every agent works from it, and you get one dashboard of sales, jobs and cash, every week.

Why Agents

A single automation does one task. An agent owns a job: it reads the customer's history, decides the next step within the rules you set, and passes the work on. You start with the agents you need and add the rest later.

You Stay in Control

Autonomous, never unattended.

You approve every campaign, and anything outside the rules you set.

Each agent says it's automated, and hands hard questions to a person.

Only opted-in contacts are emailed, and every email carries a way to stop.

Every action is logged in the CRM. Invoices stay in your accounting software.
