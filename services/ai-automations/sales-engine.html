Inside the Engine

Five parts, one record.

The Hub

Full Backend CRM

Lead forms, emails, messages, calls, quotations, sales orders and payments feed one database. Follow-ups, reminders and approvals trigger on their own, and the owner gets one dashboard: sales, jobs and cash, every week.

New

Campaign Agent

Writes the campaign in your tone, sends it to opted-in contacts once you approve, and files every reply and sign-up in the CRM.

New

Sales Order Generator

Turns a won deal or a completed form into a numbered sales order, sends it, and logs it against the customer.

Lead Follow-Up

A reply within a minute in English or Bahasa Malaysia, two or three qualifying questions, and a timed sequence that stops when they answer.

Estimate Follow-Up

A check-in on day two, an answer to the usual questions on day five, a last word on day ten. Won, lost or pending, in one list. Paid orders hand off to your accounting software, so your e-Invoice set-up stays where it is.

For Example

A training provider runs a public course. A campaign goes to past participants who opted in. Sign-ups arrive through a form, and each one becomes a numbered sales order within minutes, ready for the participant's company. Unpaid orders are chased on their own, and the owner sees every seat filled on one dashboard.
