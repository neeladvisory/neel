Does my phone number change?

No. Your main number stays. It forwards to the receptionist only when you're busy or don't answer.

Can we give it our own name?

Yes. AIRA is the name of NEEL's own receptionist. Yours is built separately and introduces itself with the name you choose.

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as an automated assistant and says the call is recorded, every time.

Does it speak Bahasa Malaysia?

Yes, English and Bahasa Malaysia. The Malay voice is switched on only after you've heard and approved a recorded test call.

What if it can't answer a question?

It doesn't guess. It takes a message, and the question shows up in your summary and the monthly report so we can add the answer.

Where do recordings and caller details go?

To your summary and your CRM. Hosted in Malaysia or Singapore under a one-page data processing agreement, and exportable if you leave.
