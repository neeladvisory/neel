What It Does

Four things, quietly.

YOUR LINE

Your number stays

We give you a Malaysian number. Your mobile forwards to it only when you are busy or do not answer. Switch forwarding off any time.

THE ASSISTANT

Says who it is

Introduces itself as your automated assistant, in English or Bahasa Malaysia, and takes the caller's name, number and message.

SCREENING

Scams never reach you

The usual scam and robocall patterns are ended politely. You see them in the summary, not on your phone.

SUMMARIES

One email per call

Who called, what about, what they want, within a minute. Recordings kept for 30 days, then deleted.

Getting Started

Live in a few days.

A short chat, a test call you approve, and the forwarding codes for your telco. Then the summaries start arriving.

  1. 01A short chat. What the assistant should say, which calls to screen, where to send the summaries.
  2. 02A test call. Hear the greeting and approve it.
  3. 03Forwarding. Set busy and no-answer forwarding to your new number; we send the dial codes.
  4. 04Live. Tell us anything to change in the first week.

House Rules

Polite, honest, on the record.

Says it is an automated assistant, every call

Tells callers the call is recorded

Never shares your diary or address, only what you approved

Your data is yours: export or delete on request
