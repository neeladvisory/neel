How work moves

From a first enquiry to an invoice.

One piece of work, handed from team to team. The agents do each step; you come in where something is signed or sent.

Pick your team

Take the departments you need.

Each department is a set of agents with one job. Start with one, add more when you are ready, and leave out what your business does not need.
