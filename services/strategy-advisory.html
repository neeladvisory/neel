What we help with

Three questions we help answer.

Bring us one question or all three. Most businesses start with the first, then come back for the next.

01 Question 01 · Plan

Where should we go?

The direction, and whether the numbers hold up.

  • Business plan and growth options
  • Financial projections, cash flow and pricing
  • Go-to-market plan

You get: a plan and a model you can take to a bank or your shareholders.

02 Question 02 · Set up

Is the business set up to get there?

How the work gets done, what it costs, and what to automate.

  • Process and SOP review
  • Supplier and cost review
  • What to automate first, built through AI & Automations

You get: a cleaner way of running the business and a ranked automation list.

03 Question 03 · Track

Is it working?

Knowing every month whether the plan is on track.

  • KPIs and a simple dashboard
  • Monthly or quarterly reviews
  • Course corrections as things change

You get: a regular check-in with people who already know the plan.
