Privacy notice

What we collect, and what we do with it.

This notice covers this website, enquiries, and the automation tools NEEL runs for clients. It is written to be read, not filed.

This website

Enquiries and visits

  • The free diagnostic form. We receive what you enter: your name, business, email, phone or WhatsApp number, the services you ticked, your question and when you need to decide. It is stored in NEEL's Google Workspace account and used only to reply and arrange the call. Kept for 12 months from your last message, then deleted, unless an engagement follows.
  • Email, phone and WhatsApp enquiries. We receive what you send, typically your name, organisation, email or phone number, and message. Used to reply and, if you ask, to scope work. Kept for 12 months from your last message, then deleted, unless an engagement follows. WhatsApp messages pass through WhatsApp under its own terms.
  • Phone calls to +603-6043 2474 are answered by AIRA, our AI voice receptionist. She takes your name, number and reason for calling, then sends NEEL a short summary. Any recording and the summary follow the same rule: 12 months, unless an engagement follows.
  • Website visits are counted without cookies or personal identifiers, so there is no cookie banner. We see which pages are read, not who reads them.

We do not add you to a mailing list, share your details outside NEEL, or send marketing because you enquired.

The tools we run

If you are a customer of a business that uses one of our tools

  • The client business decides what is collected and why. NEEL processes the data on the client's behalf, under a written data processing agreement with every client, and only for the tool's purpose.
  • Messages and calls from our tools say they are automated. Every outbound message includes a way to stop, and stop requests are honoured within one working day.
  • Marketing messages go only to people who opted in.
  • Tool data is hosted in Malaysia or Singapore. Messages pass through platforms such as email providers and WhatsApp, under those platforms' own terms.
  • Booking tools collect only booking and contact details.
  • When a client stops using a tool, its data is exported to the client and NEEL's copy is deleted.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, withdraw consent, and require that direct marketing stop. Write to support@neeladvisory.my. We reply within one working day and act within the time the Act allows. For data held inside a client's tool, we will connect you with that client or act on the client's instructions.
